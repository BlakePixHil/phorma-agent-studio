Counts tell us which parts of the studio people actually reach. A node kind nobody adds, a panel nobody opens, or a deploy target that fails more often than it succeeds are all things we cannot see from support requests alone.

In practice it decides what we fix and build next, and it tells us when a release has broken something on a platform we do not run day to day.

It is counts only, never content, and the studio works exactly the same with this off.
